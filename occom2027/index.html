01 · WHY OCCOM

Every car has cameras. Every road has LEDs.

Optical camera communication (OCC) turns this existing hardware into a communication and sensing layer that complements RF-based V2X. Event cameras, with microsecond-scale temporal resolution and high dynamic range, open vehicular optical links that conventional image sensors cannot support. Yet channel models, coding schemes, and field results are developed in separate communities. OCCoM brings them together.

Camera as perception and radio

One sensor that sees the road and receives from it, linking OCC to V2X, collective perception, and physical AI.

Theory meets the road

Information-theoretic limits and coding side by side with real-world vehicular field trials.

Three communities, one room

Optical wireless, computer vision and event-based sensing, and vehicular technology.

02 · TOPICS OF INTEREST

From photons to cooperative decisions

Theory & Coding

  • Channel modeling for image-sensor, rolling-shutter, and event-camera communication
  • Information-theoretic limits and coding for optical camera links
  • Modulation, synchronization, and detection for vehicular optical links

Receivers & Sensing

  • Learning-aided receivers and LED detection and tracking
  • Joint communication, positioning, and sensing with cameras
  • Field trials, testbeds, and open datasets

Cooperative Mobility

  • Collective perception and semantic communication for V2X
  • Human-centric safety perception and trust in V2X
  • Integration of optical and RF V2X in 6G

Topics include, but are not limited to, the above.
