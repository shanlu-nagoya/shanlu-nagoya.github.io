OCCoM 2027 · HALF-DAY WORKSHOP

Optical Camera Communications for Cooperative Mobility: V2X, Perception, and Physical AI

The same camera that perceives the road can also receive data from it. OCCoM brings together the optical wireless, computer vision, and vehicular communities to build the foundations of camera-based cooperative mobility for 6G.

Half-day workshop

01Scope

Vehicles already carry many cameras, and roads are filled with LED traffic lights, signs, and lamps. Optical camera communication (OCC) turns this existing hardware into a communication and sensing layer that complements RF-based V2X. Event cameras, with microsecond-scale temporal resolution and high dynamic range, are opening vehicular optical links that conventional image sensors cannot support.

Yet the field remains fragmented across the optical wireless, computer vision, and vehicular communities. Channel models, coding schemes, and real-world evaluations develop in parallel with little exchange, and the role of OCC in cooperative perception and physical AI systems is still unclear. This workshop aims to build shared theoretical and experimental foundations, and a research roadmap, for camera-based cooperative mobility.

Camera as perception and radio

Theory meets practice

Three communities, one venue

Optical wireless, computer vision and event-based sensing, and vehicular technology.

02Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Channel modeling for image-sensor, rolling-shutter, and event-camera communication
  • Information-theoretic limits and coding for optical camera links
  • Modulation, synchronization, and detection for vehicular optical links
  • Learning-aided receivers and LED detection and tracking
  • Joint communication, positioning, and sensing with cameras
  • Collective perception and semantic communication for V2X
  • Human-centric safety perception and trust in V2X
  • Field trials, testbeds, and open datasets
  • Integration of optical and RF V2X in 6G
